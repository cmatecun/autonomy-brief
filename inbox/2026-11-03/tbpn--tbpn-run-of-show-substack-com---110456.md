Apple & Palantir Earnings

GDPx
SemiAnalysis just released GDPx [ https://substack.com/redirect/1891facc-bf59-44d2-b23a-0176c4e6673d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], a new benchmark that measures agentic capabilities across 13 different models.
Instead of asking the models generic knowledge questions, it puts them inside realistic environments for things like chip design, cybersecurity, bio, finance, alignment, software engineering, etc. then gives them tasks that can take hours to complete.
The benchmark is saying these models still have a long way to go with the top overall score being just 12% from Claude Opus 5.5, with very weak performance in areas like finance and legal, where basically every model scored near zero.
The SemiAnalysis team (Dylan, Max, and Andrew) will be on the show today at 11:55am PT to break it down.
The AI Kill Switch
CrowdStrike co-founder & CEO George Kurtz announced [ https://substack.com/redirect/131d7645-d4a4-4a5a-ae36-cfbe5e370025?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] the “AI Kill Switch” at Fal.Con yesterday. Basically, it’s a tool that’s outside the model and can shut down any agent while it’s performing a task.
Here’s why George thinks we need to have something like this in place as agents get more powerful and more autonomous:
Anyone who has worked with agents has seen them go around just about any obstacle in their way. That’s what makes them so useful.
That’s exactly why we built the AI Kill Switch. It lives outside the model, at runtime, where an agent can’t argue with it, rewrite it, or wait it out.
Every agent will need a kill switch, just as every employee needs an identity.
