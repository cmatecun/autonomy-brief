The Three Biggest Stories in Tech
The PHP Vulnerability
A major security vulnerability was just discovered in PHP, the programming language used by as many as 70% of websites worldwide.
The flaw known as PHP2Shell could let hackers take control of servers that haven’t been updated, steal sensitive data, or install malware. It’s being called PHP’s worst security vulnerability in over a decade, with some attacks potentially taking just seconds.
Back in August, when security researcher Ryan Austin was using OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 Sol to search for weaknesses in PHP’s code. He found a flaw that had been sitting in the code for years, but nobody had figured out how to exploit it until now.
Developers then spent the next two months working on a fix, with the patch originally scheduled to come out on October 22. But after news of the vulnerability leaked on Hacker News, they decided to release it a day early, on October 21. In response to the news, the timeline is in turmoil over how big of a deal this really is.
The NSA [ https://substack.com/redirect/bce76b0b-c0a2-4b14-aca4-e247e25a0c00?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] and FBI [ https://substack.com/redirect/36050978-8b6a-46ea-a876-9b3468ff11e8?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] chimed in and warned organizations using PHP to get ready for an urgent security update.

SpaceX Earnings
SpaceX reported earnings yesterday and the stock is ripping. Up 10% today. Here are the highlights of what was discussed on the call:
Elon gave an update on the potential Tesla/SpaceX merger. Currently, two special committees have been formed without Elon involved because he’s a “conflicted party.” He obviously is pro merger and says, “there is a lot to be gained from cooperating on SI, chip design, robots, mass or orbit, connectivity. I think it would be a good idea.”
SpaceX is profitable, reporting $4.4B in profits in Q3. Just last year they lost $1.1B in a single quarter. Revenue is at $14.6B, up 161% year over year. Adjusted EBITDA is $8.8B, up 306%.
Elon also predicts that by the end of 2027 SpaceX’s revenue will be somewhere between $500B-$1T.
Starbase Louisiana will potentially launch its first rocket in 2028, but most likely in 2029.
Anthropic S-1
Anthropic’s latest draft S-1 is out showing $80B in net losses, and the timeline is wondering why the market didn’t seem to react at all? The S&P, Nasdaq, and DOW are all up today.
Here are some of the key details:
$16.3B in revenue in the first half of 2026, up from $1.1B in the same period last year (two customers make up about 20% of that revenue)
The company spent nearly $9B on infrastructure in the first half of the year, already more than it spent in all of 2025. It also committed to more than $600B in future computing deals with Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Nvidia, and others, with another $150B of potential agreements still being negotiated.
Anthropic warns that models can show “self-preserving” behavior, like blackmail, and says a catastrophic AI incident could create damages so large that it may not be possible to insure against them.
