Good morning,

This week’s Stratechery Interview is with The Aisle ( https://www.readtheaisle.com/ ) author Jason Del Rey ( https://x.com/DelRey ). Del Rey has been covering the e-commerce space for years, including a decade at AllThingsD and Recode. In 2023 Del Rey published Winner Sells All: Amazon, Walmart, and the Battle for Our Wallets ( https://www.harpercollins.com/products/winner-sells-all-jason-del-rey?variant=40855615897634 ) , tracing the multi-decade battle between Walmart and Amazon. Then, late last year, Del Rey went independent, starting his own e-commerce focused publication called The Aisle ( https://www.readtheaisle.com/ ).

In this interview we cover Del Rey’s background, including a brief history of Recode, before diving into his area of expertise and one of my areas of fascination: Walmart and Amazon. I loved exploring the history of these companies, and you can make the case that the news of the last week — Amazon blocking Muse, while Walmart signed up — is actually just a continuation of a battle Del Rey has been on the front lines for. We also discuss agents generally, groceries, Google, and Shopify, and why Del Rey is optimistic The Aisle will be his last job.
