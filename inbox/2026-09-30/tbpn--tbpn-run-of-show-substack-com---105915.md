Dollars per Watt
Great discussion featuring Elon Musk, Jensen Huang and Gavin Baker yesterday at the America.gov [ https://substack.com/redirect/4eb920b0-ee1f-41dc-b29e-4173dd97d087?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] summit.
Here’s the key section of the transcript:
Elon Musk: Just to frame this: the average power consumption in the United States is about 500 gigawatts.
So every 5 gigawatts of incremental steady-state production is a 1% increase in the power used.
And I think that will actually materialize as approximately a 1% increase in GDP, because the intelligence per watt keeps increasing.
If you plot how useful intelligence per watt is increasing, it’s happening at both levels: at the hardware level, Jensen’s GPUs keep getting better in terms of compute per watt, and the algorithms also keep getting better at producing more intelligence per watt.
So I think that’s an interesting point. I would bet anyone that a 1% increase in power usage corresponds to roughly a 1% increase in GDP.
And therefore 10 gigawatts would be a 2% increase in GDP.
Gavin Baker: Let’s do the simple math. Each gigawatt can probably generate, call it, $40 or $50 billion of economic output each year.
Elon Musk: Well, 1% of the United States—
Gavin Baker: About $300 billion.
Elon Musk: Yeah. So 5 gigawatts would be $300 billion.
Gavin Baker: Exactly. So about $60 billion per gigawatt.
Elon Musk: Yeah, that’s right.
Gavin Baker: The math maths.
So there are a bunch of interesting stats flowing downstream of this math. It’s actually convenient that “giga” is a billion, so just as a gigawatt is a billion watts, a gigadollar would be a billion dollars. The average electrical load in the United States is indeed around 500GW, it was actually closer to 479 GW average in 2025, but ~500GW is a very reasonable round number. US GDP for 2025 was roughly $30.8 trillion. So the simple math is America currently produces roughly $60–65 billion of GDP per continuously consumed gigawatt.
What’s interesting is that the frontier labs are tracking almost perfectly in line with this, both Anthropic and OpenAI are reportedly sitting between $60b and $70b in terms of ARR, and both of them have around 1 GW live (although way more has been contracted).
Now, two important notes. First, this is obviously just a moment in time snapshot. The labs have contracted way more compute, and if revenue doesn’t increase proportionally, the ratio falls dramatically. On the flip slide, Elon’s point about “useful intelligence per watt increasing” could increase this ratio significantly to the upside.
The second factor is that GDP ≠ revenue. GDP measures value added. OpenAI/Anthropic revenue includes payments that ultimately pay Nvidia, AWS, Oracle, electricity generators, employees, etc. If you calculated the labs’ actual value added, the number would be substantially lower.
But it’s a fascinating coincidence that, right at the moment it feels like we are entering the AGI era, one continuously powered watt in the American economy supports about $65/year of GDP and one watt of frontier AI compute is supporting roughly $65/year of AI revenue.
