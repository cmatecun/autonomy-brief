Good morning,

This week’s Stratechery Interview is with Joanna Stern. Stern has a long history of covering technology, including a long run as the Senior Personal Technology Columnist at the Wall Street Journal; earlier this year Stern went independent: she has a newsletter called New Things with Joanna Stern ( https://thenewthings.com/ ) , and a YouTube Channel ( https://www.youtube.com/c/joannastern ) that is exploding in popularity. That’s hardly a surprise: Stern makes some of the most creative and entertaining videos in tech, including her just-posted battery test of the new iPhone 18 Pro ( https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YNqYEMuoQAI ).

I spoke to Stern fairly recently, in May ( https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-joanna-stern-about-living-with-ai/?access_token=eyJhbGciOiJSUzI1NiIsImtpZCI6InN0cmF0ZWNoZXJ5LnBhc3Nwb3J0Lm9ubGluZSIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJhdWQiOiJzdHJhdGVjaGVyeS5wYXNzcG9ydC5vbmxpbmUiLCJhenAiOiJIS0xjUzREd1Nod1AyWURLYmZQV00xIiwiZW50Ijp7InVyaSI6WyJodHRwczovL3N0cmF0ZWNoZXJ5LmNvbS8yMDI2L2FuLWludGVydmlldy13aXRoLWpvYW5uYS1zdGVybi1hYm91dC1saXZpbmctd2l0aC1haS8iXX0sImV4cCI6MTc5MjIzMTM2MiwiaWF0IjoxNzg5NjM5MzYyLCJpc3MiOiJodHRwczovL2FwcC5wYXNzcG9ydC5vbmxpbmUvb2F1dGgiLCJzY29wZSI6ImZlZWQ6cmVhZCBhcnRpY2xlOnJlYWQgYXNzZXQ6cmVhZCBjYXRlZ29yeTpyZWFkIGVudGl0bGVtZW50cyIsInN1YiI6IjAxOTUxNmYxLWEzNDYtNzYxNC05ZTk2LWI2M2JkMTFmZTg1NiIsInVzZSI6ImFjY2VzcyJ9.iyWD3-YBntzzpvvS2JA61_Hxj-3MaIVZAB2dqG_pXPI2Dj1QIm1cHnnzv2Js2opCD0ASewJk-fFBEZvUSZ0hnW836RnrY0oVS6TQaoTx7A4KNN4PLDuZnkulujBe9ghxI5jRKMvCjhkw8Z4JV_WtoLEjZkEFRaf9Ht86GhmNBoaaBq89KLmpIF4PmkP2N6RDzN75N8qpDFxaC5BV-xP_0UIZuuob-IYBs4d3YXlt0j9olvQRG8fh1GuJorYvPY-6WzVyQoR3mkvqxI5QF41jRSKoti5k402kAXMuLTpq3qCR4Tchecdpdf3nCeXIhc634oNBAZ2IizmGz7iJ-3-bMQ ) , about her excellent new book I Am Not a Robot: My Year Using AI to Do (Almost) Everything ( https://joannastern.com/ ) ; one of the topics we discuss in this Interview is what she would change now that Agents actually work. We also have an extended discussion about whether or not normal people will ever use agents, and how tech goes wrong by not understanding those normal people.

The reason to have Stern on now, however, is that it is gadget season, and Apple has a new gadget: the iPhone Duo. We talk about Stern’s experience with the phone, and concern that it might end up being a bit of a parlor trick. We also talk about the return of home labs and experimentation, our love of computers, and why that sets us apart from doomers filling people with fear.
