Waymo has opened its next-generation robotaxi to all riders in Los Angeles, Phoenix, and San Francisco. For now, customers in these markets may be matched with the new robotaxi, called the Ojai (pronounced oh-hi) when they hail a ride. Once Waymo has enough Ojais in its fleet, riders will be able to choose between the new vehicle and the older Jaguar I-Pace robotaxi.

‘I drove Tesla FSD, Rivian Autonomy+ ‘hands-free’ driving systems. Here’s how they compare’ (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/12502f98-2f9b-481d-a48c-fbe34358d503?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]). This is a fair assessment of Rivian’s UHF capabilities. Rivian makes a great car, but its driver-assistance system still feels about 5–7 years behind Tesla FSD Supervised today. But that doesn’t mean it will take Rivian another 5–7 years to close the gap. Rivian is working with a more robust sensor stack, cheaper and more powerful compute, and the benefit of years of industry-wide advances in AI and autonomy. The bigger question is how quickly Rivian can turn those advantages into a system that actually matches Tesla on the road.
“I hope that we can even exceed the Tesla system because of our investments in a more robust sensor stack,” Rivian’s Philbin said. “We’re actually a big believer in multimodal imaging, so cameras and radars, and later on, early next year, lidar as well on the R2.”

Einride to deploy 500 Tesla Semis in biggest electric truck order yet (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/d463e5b9-5783-4dd9-b4ed-7d6d28a07501?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).
Einride is putting 500 Tesla Semis on US roads. That will take the Swedish freight company from roughly 250 deployed electric trucks to about 750. First deliveries start next month, with the rest arriving in phases over 24 months. Five states are in scope: California, Texas, New Jersey, Illinois, and Georgia. Amazon is the named customer, and every truck gets managed through Saga, Einride’s software platform for routing and charging windows.
