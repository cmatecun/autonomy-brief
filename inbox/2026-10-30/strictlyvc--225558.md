## Top News

**Tech stocks** were hammered Friday as investors grew more skeptical about the enormous sums being spent on AI, sending the Nasdaq Composite down 2.9%. **Intel** plunged 29% in its worst day since the 1970s, **Amazon** fell 1.4% after an earnings miss, **Nvidia** dropped 4%, **Broadcom** sank more than 5%, and **Qualcomm** tumbled 11%, while **Alphabet** extended losses after plunging more than 12% Thursday. CNBC has [more here](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/intel-stock-has-worst-day-since-1970s-wiping-about-12-billion-off-governments-stake.html).

**Blackstone** has told investors that **Anthropic** may receive a high-yield or **“junk” credit rating** after its IPO rather than the investment-grade rating previously expected, potentially raising the AI company’s borrowing costs and narrowing the pool of investors able to finance its planned infrastructure spending. The Financial Times has [more here](https://www.ft.com/content/59383c7f-8d82-493f-bf01-d2e862a1018f).

**North Korean hackers** are being blamed for stealing at least $6.3 billion in crypto from **Binance** after compromising an engineer’s laptop, in what researchers are calling the largest crypto theft on record; Binance says roughly $3 billion has since been frozen and that user funds remain safe. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/north-korean-hackers-blamed-for-stealing-record-6-3b-in-binance-breach/).

**OpenAI** has secured roughly $11 billion in commitments for its latest financing – less than half of the $30 billion it initially targeted – at a $1.25 trillion valuation versus the $1.4 trillion it had hoped for. The company is now aiming to raise up to $20 billion as **investor enthusiasm** for the AI boom cools. The Wall Street Journal has [more here](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openai-raises-less-than-half-of-30-billion-target-in-latest-round-91aecdc5).

## Pile-Ins, Payouts, and Pacing Pressure: The Investors Behind the VCs Open Up

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/ccd8eccf-5143-414c-a1b8-2cc7ddf4c6f8/Beezer-and-Amit.jpeg?t=1793420556)
Caption: Image Credits: TechCrunch / StrictlyVC

**By Connie Loizos**

The limited partners who provide the capital to venture firms — the “money behind the money” — are feeling great right now. They’re in uncharted territory in many ways, but also potentially exceedingly lucrative territory.

Normally, LPs typically spread their bets across many firms to diversify their own portfolios. They write checks to emerging managers and established ones, to generalist firms and specialists. The goal is a strong return, but also to minimize risk.

That’s the typical playbook. But typical is out the window. OpenAI and Anthropic’s valuations have shot up so quickly that nearly everyone has piled into the same two companies on the way up, often inside the same venture firm, despite the longstanding venture convention of avoiding investments that conflict with one another. No one is complaining, either. The LPs I speak with seem unbothered by this big gamble, given the potential returns; they’re expecting a bonanza.

But they do see danger ahead, even if they describe this as a “yellow light” situation, and they’re uneasy about how quickly VCs are coming back to raise new funds.

I talked about all of it recently with Beezer Clarkson of LGT Capital Partners, a Zurich-based diversified asset manager, and Amit Bhatti of TrueBridge, a Chapel Hill firm that focuses solely on venture. This conversation has been edited for length.

**These are strange days — exciting but also scary. How are you feeling about your roles right now, and your place in this startup ecosystem?**

Beezer Clarkson: It’s very busy, but I wouldn’t say it’s scary. There’s a lot of change and a lot of variables at play. I love venture, so I find the chaos fun. I’ve been doing this for a couple of decades, so seeing the cycles makes it less scary. You appreciate that these things happen, and that we should enjoy the upside, because sometimes there’s downside, and the up is a lot more fun than the down.

## Massive Fundings

**&Truth**, a Palo Alto startup founded this year that develops tools that mathematically verify whether legacy and AI-generated code meets specified safety, security, and correctness requirements, raised a $70 million seed round. The New York Times has [_more here_](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/29/science/ai-eradicate-computer-bugs.html).

**Baseten**, a seven-year-old San Francisco startup that provides infrastructure for businesses to run AI models, is reportedly set to raise about $2 billion in a round led by previous investor **Sands Capital**, with separate tranches valuing the company at $26 billion and $30 billion. Bloomberg has [more here](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-30/baseten-set-for-30-billion-valuation-with-new-funding).

**Powerlattice**, a three-year-old startup based in Vancouver, WA, that makes chiplets that deliver power inside AI processor packages to reduce energy loss, has sold $86 million of a planned $111 million private financing, according to an SEC filing. GeekWire has [more here](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/filing-chip-startup-powerlattice-raises-86m-to-ease-ais-power-crunch/).

**Setara Biosciences**, a San Diego startup founded this year that develops small-molecule treatments targeting the genetic causes of rare neurological and neuromuscular diseases, raised a $90 million Series A round. Investors included **Jazz Pharmaceuticals** as well as previous investors **Deerfield Management**, **Canaan**, **Droia Ventures**, **EcoR1**, **Euclidean Capital**, and **Regeneron Ventures**. Fierce Biotech has [_more here_](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/setara-raises-90m-series-carrying-actio-torch-following-jazz-acquisition).

## Big-But-Not-Crazy-Big Fundings

**Allox**, a three-year-old Barcelona startup that combines genetics, experimental biology, and AI to identify protein sites that can be targeted with small-molecule drugs, raised a $10.5 million seed round led by **Asabys**, with **CDTI** and **Institut Català de Finances** also stepping up. European Biotechnology has [_more here_](https://european-biotechnology.com/latest-news/barcelonas-allox-raises-e9-4m-to-turn-protein-maps-into-drug-candidates/).

**Braid**, a three-year-old startup based in The Hague, Netherlands, that lets enterprises embed branded SIM and eSIM connectivity into their products through a single connection to hundreds of carriers, raised a $38 million Series A round led by **Octopus Ventures**, with **KPN Ventures** also digging in. EU-Startups has [_more here_](https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/10/profitable-dutch-startup-braid-raises-e34-12-to-help-brands-launch-their-own-mobile-connectivity/).

**Ravical**, a one-year-old startup based in Ghent, Belgium, that uses AI to turn accounting and tax firms' expertise into standardized workflows across their client bases, raised a $17 million seed round led by **MMC Ventures**, with **Shapers** as well as previous investors **Lakestar** and **Pitchdrive** also investing. The company has raised a total of $25 million. [Tech.eu](https://Tech.eu) has [_more here_](https://tech.eu/2026/10/29/ravical-raises-17m-to-break-the-link-between-accounting-firms-growth-and-hiring/).

**Wrapped Health**, a one-year-old startup based in Washington, DC, that integrates with electronic health records so clinicians can send patients product recommendations and purchasing options, raised a $2.1 million pre-seed round co-led by **Lightbank** and **Arteria Capital**. **Pope Leo XIV** is pushing back against calls to grant AI systems rights or legal personhood, arguing that “machines are tools, not persons” and warning that treating them otherwise could obscure human responsibility when AI causes harm; he also reiterated his call to “disarm” AI and keep decisions involving human life and the use of force under human control. His [post on X](https://x.com/Pontifex/status/2116146977230459383) is here, and Futurism has [more here](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/pope-leo-ai-personhood).

Puck has [more here](https://puck.news/ai/2026/10/pro-ai-pacs-are-retreating-from-the-midterms).
