Top Story of the Week
Waymo taps Toyota to supply robotaxis for Japan and the U.K. (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/f854ad3c-b3c5-45c4-8df7-17423e3799ae?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]). This is a big step forward from the preliminary agreement Toyota and Waymo announced [ https://substack.com/redirect/0b062529-2406-4833-9eba-273025e468b1?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] last year. Toyota won’t just supply the vehicles, it will also integrate Waymo’s self-driving hardware directly at its factory in Japan. That could make it cheaper and easier to produce robotaxis at scale.
The deal also gives Waymo another vehicle option as it expands internationally. With AV operators like Motional and Avride also using the Hyundai IONIQ 5 for their robotaxis, it will be interesting to see how companies differentiates themselves if they’re all using the same vehicle. Waymo, meanwhile, is building out a diverse fleet with the Jaguar I-PACE, Zeekr Ojai, Hyundai IONIQ 5, and now Toyota bZ4X, giving it more flexibility across different markets.
And there’s potentially a much bigger opportunity here too, as Toyota and Waymo are still exploring bringing Waymo’s self-driving technology to personally owned vehicles.
Waymo will begin testing autonomous Toyota bZ4X vehicles in Japan next year as part of a broader partnership with the automaker to supply robotaxis for international markets with right-hand-drive vehicles, including the U.K. Under the partnership announced Friday, Toyota will manufacture the 2027 bZ4X vehicles and integrate Waymo’s self-driving system directly at its Takaoka factory in Japan.
