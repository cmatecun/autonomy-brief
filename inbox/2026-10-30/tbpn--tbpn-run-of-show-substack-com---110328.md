Binance & Polymarket Hacked
Binance and Polymarket were both hit by major hacks within hours of each other yesterday, with roughly $6.3B stolen from Binance and another $2.7M from Polymarket. Here’s what happened:
Binance
Hackers prepared 36 hours before draining $6.3B from Binance-controlled wallets across at least 10 blockchains, with most of the money moving in about 20 minutes. Blockchain analytics firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis have accused TraderTraitor, an elite North Korean hacking operation, for the hack, although Binance and law enforcement have not confirmed that. Around $2.8B of the stolen funds has already been frozen, and Binance says it will cover all customer losses from the attack.
Polymarket
Polymarket’s hack was smaller and worked differently. The company says its main platform, Polymarket US, was not breached. Instead, attackers compromised a third-party vendor in the UK and used that access to steal money from some users’ wallets. Around 700 users were affected. Trading was briefly paused, and Polymarket says it will reimburse everyone who lost money.

The Ghost of J.P. Morgan (1896)
J.P. Morgan frequently stayed at the exclusive Jekyll Island Club in Georgia, where he reportedly enjoyed smoking cigars on his private porch early in the morning. According to local legend, guests staying in his former apartment still wake up to the smell of cigar smoke, even when nobody is smoking.
The Computer Virus That Demanded $189 (1989)
The AIDS Trojan, one of the earliest known ransomware attacks, was distributed on floppy disks mailed to thousands of people. After the computer had been started 90 times, it hid users’ files and demanded $189 be sent to a post office box in Panama to restore access.
The AI-Generated Woman Who Kept Coming Back (2022)
In 2022, artist Steph Maj Swanson discovered a creepy AI-generated woman she named “Loab” while experimenting with an image generator. The same haunting face kept reappearing in new images, often surrounded by disturbing and violent imagery, earning Loab the nickname “the first AI-generated cryptid.”
