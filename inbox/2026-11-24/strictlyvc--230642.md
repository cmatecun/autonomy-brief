## Yale’s Sonnenfeld: Nvidia Is a ‘Detriment’ to U.S. Industrial Might — Not a Champion

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/0498ddde-8892-4eed-a583-9e276ec17c34/Jensen-ChatGPT.jpeg?t=1795573744)
Caption: Image Credits: ChatGPT for TechCrunch

**By Connie Loizos**

Investors cheered Jensen Huang’s return as Nvidia’s CEO on Monday, but renowned business academic Jeffrey Sonnenfeld of Yale thinks they’re celebrating a mistake. He sees a company that is largely responsible for putting sophisticated technology in the hands of the country’s adversaries, at a moment when the risks [_couldn’t be higher_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/anthropics-prospectus-details-losses-growth-and-yes-a-warning-that-its-ai-could-end-humanity/). Far from a champion of American industry, he says, Nvidia is “a detriment to U.S. global industrial might.”

As evidence, Sonnenfeld points to research suggesting Nvidia’s [_most advanced chips_](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/nvidias-powerful-b300-chips-spotted-chinese-state-owned-network?rc=omehqc) have reached China despite U.S. export controls, and to [_sinking shares_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/anthropic-shares-fall-below-ipo-price-a-worrying-sign-for-ai-startups/) of Anthropic, which he links to the realization that China’s seemingly miraculous open-source AI models are, in a sense, copies of top U.S. labs’ work, even as Nvidia has insisted that smuggling is a “non-factor.”

Indeed, Huang’s return was [_greeted with just as much enthusiasm_](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/11/24/king-is-back-huangs-return-nvidia-is-greeted-with-relief-asia/) in Asia, The Washington Post reported. “The king is back!” read one headline from China’s state-affiliated Star Market Daily.

The reinstatement itself followed heavy pressure from Washington. Nvidia’s chairman had effectively ruled out Huang’s return just last week. Then, in a torrent of private weekend calls led by Vice President JD Vance, top administration officials leaned on the board to welcome Huang back, with President Trump calling the board directly to vouch for him. “[_I made the deal_](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html),” Trump told CNBC on Monday.

Sonnenfeld says the directors who went along, and who returned Huang to the board even as it investigates whether foreign intelligence efforts targeted him, now face a “historic level of individual liability.”

Read more (https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/yales-sonnenfeld-nvidia-is-a-detriment-to-u-s-industrial-might-not-a-champion/)
