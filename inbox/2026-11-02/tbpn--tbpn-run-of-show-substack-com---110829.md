Fridges Are Getting Hacked
Hackers [ https://substack.com/redirect/e5afe891-df5f-4b61-aebe-570ad1144548?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] are remotely taking control of supermarket refrigerators and freezers across Europe, turning up their temperatures and forcing stores to throw away thousands of dollars worth of food.
The attacks have affected countries like Sweden, Belgium, Luxembourg, Estonia, Ireland the Netherlands, and Finland across chains including IKEA, Carrefour, ICA, Coop, and Ahold Delhaize.
- Sweden: One ICA supermarket reported that its freezers reached 122°F, destroying its frozen inventory.
- Belgium: A butcher reported that hackers raised his refrigerators to 64°F, ruining roughly €15,000 worth of meat.
- Ireland: 13 SuperValu stores were affected, but the issue is now resolved for those stores
- Carrefour: 15 of its 700 Belgian stores were affected.
Katrien Eggers, spokesperson for Belgium’s Centre for Cybersecurity, says no one knows who is behind these attacks and that we may never know. And since no one has demanded a ransom, there’s growing suspicion that the goal is simply to cause damage rather than make money.
Strategy Risk’s Melissa Chen thinks the rise of these kinds of hacks is a good reason to hold on to older appliances that aren’t connected to the internet.
