## Anthropic Warns That It’s Very Close to RSI Capability, Aka Self-Building AI

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/e7ffedc1-0944-4f37-815b-2af841407b22/Recursive-Neural-Spiral-in-Circuitry__1_.jpeg?t=1793659459)
Caption: Image Credits: TechCrunch / AI-generated

**By Julie Bort**

While much attention has focused on the updated financials Anthropic revealed [_in its revised S-1_](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026068676/anthropicpbc-sx1ax1.htm#i199fd6a8a0664d5da497db9a78cf0452_55)/[_A _](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026068676/anthropicpbc-sx1ax1.htm#i199fd6a8a0664d5da497db9a78cf0452_55)on Monday (revenue is soaring, but so are expenses), the AI lab dropped another little bombshell in the fine print.

The filing seems to warn that the company is on the cusp of creating a model capable of recursive self-improvement (RSI).

RSI is the point at which AI gets so good at training itself that it spawns a dramatic acceleration of AI capabilities. To the AI doomers, RSI represents the cataclysm from which there is no return, in which AI builds itself into something powerful enough that it slips beyond human control and may be indifferent to the damage it causes while achieving its goals.

To AI enthusiasts, RSI, or some version of AI reliably using itself to improve, represents the technology’s promise, where it becomes a tool of unimaginable productivity, scientific discovery, and more.

Anthropic seems to believe that RSI is both a promise and a peril. It says in the S-1 that it uses AI in its R&D, but it cautions: “Using AI to help build more capable AI could also pose a considerable safety risk, particularly if it sharply accelerates the pace of progress. This is one of the areas covered by our Responsible Scaling Policy, which sets out our plans to monitor and mitigate the risks as we approach what has been termed ‘recursive self-improvement.’”

When the term [_RSI became popular earlier this year_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/rsi-is-the-new-agi-and-its-just-as-hard-to-pin-down/), many believed it was a buzzword or, at best, a goal too hard to achieve, define, or prove.

## Massive Fundings

**Casap**, a four-year-old New York startup that uses AI agents to automate fraud investigations, disputes, collections, claims, and operational exceptions for financial institutions, raised a $56 million Series B round led by **Base10 Partners**, with **Experian** and **Synchrony** as well as previous investors **Emergence Capital** and **Lightspeed Venture Partners** also investing. The company has raised a total of $90 million. [_More here_](https://pulse2.com/casap-raises-56-million-series-b-to-expand-agentic-ai-platform-for-financial-services/).

**Hypersonica**, a three-year-old startup based in Feldkirchen, Germany, that develops hypersonic long-range missile systems for European governments and allied countries, raised a $111.8 million Series B round at an approximately $783 million valuation. The deal was co-led by **Lightspeed** and **General Catalyst**, with previous investors **Plural** and **201 Ventures** also pitching in. The company has raised a total of approximately $140.9 million. Tech Funding News has [_more here_](https://techfundingnews.com/hypersonica-111m-series-b-lightspeed-general-catalyst/).

**Northwood Space**, a four-year-old startup based in Torrance, CA, that designs satellite ground-station antennas and software that let operators buy capacity on a shared communications network, raised a $375 million Series C round at a $2 billion post-money valuation. The deal was co-led by **Andreessen Horowitz** and **DFJ Growth**, with **Alpine Space Ventures**, **Washington Harbour Partners**, **Stepstone Activate**, and **In-Q-Tel** also taking part. It also secured a separate multi-year credit facility from **HSBC Innovation Banking**. TechCrunch has [_more here_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/northwood-space-raises-375m-series-c-at-a-2b-valuation-from-a16z-and-dfj-growth/).

**Tutor Intelligence**, a five-year-old Boston-area startup that builds robots for warehouse and manufacturing work that learn from remote human operators and share improvements across the fleet, raised a $75 million Series B led by **Andreessen Horowitz**, with previous investors **Union Square Ventures** and **Neo** also chipping in. The company has raised a total of $117 million. Forbes has [more here](https://www.forbes.com/sites/johnwerner/2026/11/02/tutor-intelligence-why-not-just-teach-the-robots-ourselves/).

## Going Public

**Anthropic** reported $17 billion in third-quarter revenue, 12 times its year-earlier total and up 47% from the second quarter, while posting a $23 billion net loss and disclosing roughly $895 billion in commitments for data centers, chips, and computing power as it prepares for an IPO that could value the company at as much as $2 trillion. The New York Times has [more here](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/anthropic-investors-push-for-cautious-valuation-closer-to-1-5-trillion-2f2df9cf).

Speaking of **Anthropic**, institutional investors are pushing the company to target a valuation of roughly $1.5 trillion to $1.6 trillion in its planned IPO later this month, down from earlier expectations approaching $2 trillion, amid the recent tech selloff and growing concern about heavy AI exposure and infrastructure spending. The Wall Street Journal has [more here](https://www.wsj.com/finance/stocks/anthropic-investors-push-for-cautious-valuation-closer-to-1-5-trillion-2f2df9cf?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

And there’s still more on **Anthropic**: it turns out it spent $4.8 billion on employee compensation and benefits during the first nine months of 2026, equal to about 15% of its revenue. In the third quarter alone, those costs averaged more than $100,000 per employee per month. Business Insider has [more here](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-ipo-filing-q3-personnel-costs-2026-11).

**OPay**, an eight-year-old Nigerian fintech, abruptly withdrew its $304 million U.S. IPO after an undisclosed foreign regulatory inquiry, hours after pricing its shares at $19 apiece for a valuation of just over $4 billion. OPay has raised $570 million in total venture capital funding from investors such as SoftBank Vision Fund, Meituan, IDG Capital, Source Code Capital, and HSG. Semafor has [more here](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/02/2026/opays-withdrawal-of-304m-us-ipo-casts-shadow-over-african-fintech).

## Layoffs

**Squarespace**, the 22-year-old website-building and hosting company, is laying off roughly 19% of its workforce, or about 300 employees, as it restructures amid rapid changes in the website-building market driven by generative AI and “vibe-coding” tools. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/web-host-squarespace-lays-off-19-of-its-employees/).

The Register has [more here](https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/11/02/suse-to-cut-one-in-ten-employees-because-of-ai/5306950).
