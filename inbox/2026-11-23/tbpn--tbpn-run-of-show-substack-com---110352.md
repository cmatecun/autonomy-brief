Jensen’s Back
The NVIDIA board of directors has officially and unanimously reinstated Jensen as CEO, President, and Board Director after his resignation 11 days ago. The timeline is going crazy after the breaking news:
An interesting piece of the story is just how involved the White House was in Jensen’s return. NVIDIA says the board received input from senior U.S. government officials and decided that bringing Jensen back posed no national security risk, while Trump said on CNBC this morning, “If I didn’t get involved, you’d probably have a very sad Nvidia for a long time to come.” He also reportedly [ https://substack.com/redirect/7fda1df9-e478-4ea3-a9b3-888b887a5bbd?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] dialed into NVIDIA’s board meeting to personally push for Jensen’s reinstatement.
As of 10am PT, the stock is up 30%. You can see the Jensen pop. Other companies are also benefitting from Jensen’s return. CoreWeave and Nebius are both up 22% today, Intel is up 13%, TSMC is up 15%, Sandisk is up 14%, and the Nasdaq is up 7%.
Here’s Jensen’s statement:
I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution. I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment. I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process. I am especially grateful to Harvey Jones for his leadership as Chair, and to Colette Kress and our executive staff for leading NVIDIA during this period. I am also deeply thankful to our employees, shareholders, leaders across the industry and partners around the world for their extraordinary support. Above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family. I can’t wait to get back to work.
