In today’s episode, I’m speaking with Jason Fiber [ https://substack.com/redirect/47da8b0f-17ef-4e2b-a552-8b5a70d9a5af?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], CEO of Virewirx [ https://substack.com/redirect/66e00814-6f0c-4be9-8506-ab8445fcef74?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. Jason introduces the company and its background in wireless technology, explaining how its VX60 wireless platform compares to other connectivity options and where it fits into autonomous vehicle operations.
We dive into the data demands of AV fleets, from how much information vehicles need to upload to why moving that data efficiently becomes increasingly important as fleets grow. Jason discusses how Virewirx approaches those challenges and how faster data transfers can fit alongside charging and other depot operations to reduce turnaround times and improve vehicle utilization.
The conversation also explores the factors that influence depot locations, the partners and operators Virewirx works with, and when high-speed data transfer becomes critical during AV development and deployment. We discuss how faster access to vehicle data could benefit cities through incident and hazard reporting.
Finally, we look beyond robotaxis to other industries that could benefit from Virewirx’s technology and discuss what’s next for the company.
