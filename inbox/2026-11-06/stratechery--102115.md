I spend a lot of time trying to figure out what is happening to businesses on the internet. What I often find I’m missing is a view across the economy itself. With more than 1.7% of global GDP running on Stripe, working with them means I can bring some of what they’re seeing to Stratechery. They are, in a sense, a barometer of the economy.

Take the “SaaSpocalypse”. Earlier this year, nearly a trillion dollars in enterprise software value disappeared amid fears of AI eating its lunch. Stock prices only tell us what investors think will happen, and not necessarily what is happening. In this case, Stripe recently built a SaaS Index ( https://www.stripeeconomics.com/p/the-saaspocalypse-was-more-like-a?utm_source=stratechery&utm_campaign=paid-newsletter& ) using payments data from 72,000 non-AI SaaS businesses. Looking at their actual performance, we can see that while SaaS stocks were selling off, revenue growth was actually accelerating.

Year-over-year growth is now over 30%, with some of the fastest growth coming from the youngest SaaS companies. I suspect there are a lot more interesting things hiding in Stripe’s data, and I’m excited about having an excuse to go look for them.
