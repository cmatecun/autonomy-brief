## Crusoe Sells Troubled Wyoming Data Center Project to Google

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/9816078b-a57a-4d82-8d09-897d17283967/crusoe-ceo-getty.jpeg?t=1794025965)
Caption: Image Credits: Brett Coomer / Houston Chronicle / Getty Images

**By Marina Temkin**

Crusoe, an eight-year-old, Denver-based data center developer recently valued at [_$30.9 billion_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/crusoe-raises-3-9b-to-build-massive-data-centers-and-small-modular-ai-factories/),[_ confirmed_](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-wyoming-data-center-campus-update) Friday it has sold an unfinished data center project in southeastern Wyoming to Google.

Google, which Crusoe had tried and failed to land as a customer for the project, is taking over the development after reportedly raising concerns about its cost and construction timeline under Crusoe’s management.

Crusoe initially announced the project last year with Blackstone-backed infrastructure company Tallgrass, planning to eventually scale it to 10 gigawatts of power capacity.

Google did not disclose how much it paid Crusoe for the unfinished project, but it announced  plans to [_invest $17 billion_](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/wyoming-data-center/) to develop the data center campus.

The deal highlights how difficult it can be for independent data center developers to deliver massive projects on time and within budget, given the challenges of securing equipment and power. Growing opposition from local communities can further complicate development.

Despite taking over the Wyoming facility, Google hasn’t walked away from working with Crusoe entirely. Crusoe is developing [_another data_](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-developing-data-center-campus-in-armstrong-county-texas) center for Google near Amarillo, Texas.

The deal to sell the site to Google was first reported [_by Bloomberg_](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/crusoe-pushed-aside-at-wyoming-ai-project-after-google-concerns) in June, but the Wyoming setback didn’t stop Crusoe from raising a [_$3.9 billion round_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/crusoe-raises-3-9b-to-build-massive-data-centers-and-small-modular-ai-factories/) in September.

Read more (https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/crusoe-sells-troubled-wyoming-data-center-project-to-google/)

## Massive Fundings

**CesiumAstro**, a nine-year-old Austin startup that develops communications systems, satellites, and space infrastructure for commercial, government, and defense customers, raised a $630 million Series D round at a $4.5+ billion post-money valuation. The deal was led by **Janus Henderson**, with **Insight Partners**, **Sands Capital**, **Sumitomo**, **Woven Capital**, **Incharge Capital**, **Magnetar**, **AV**, and **NewSpace Capital** also opting in. It also secured a $100 million debt facility provided by the Export-Import Bank of the United States and JPMorgan. The company has raised a total of $1.2+ billion in 2026. Payload has [_more here_](https://payloadspace.com/cesiumastro-raises-730m-reaches-valuation-above-4-5b/).

**Cyera**, a five-year-old New York cybersecurity startup whose software identifies sensitive corporate data and flags security gaps, is reportedly planning to raise around $800 million in a round led by previous investor **Cyberstarts** at a $20 billion valuation, up from $12 billion in June. The Information has [more here](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/cyeras-800-million-haul-shows-cyber-fever-broken).

**Future Secure AI**, a three-year-old Austin and Sydney startup that develops human-supervised AI agents that handle complex business processes for large organizations, raised $82 million in convertible notes with a reported $1.2 billion valuation cap. Investors included **Square Peg**, **Yarra Capital Management**, **Macquarie Asset Management**, and **Firetrail**. The Australian Financial Review has [_more here_](https://www.afr.com/technology/macquarie-backed-ai-start-up-hits-1-7b-valuation-readies-asx-float-20261106-p61bmc).

**Monzo**, an 11-year-old London digital bank with more than 16 million customers, is reportedly exploring a new venture funding round at roughly a $10.1 billion valuation after talks over a £10 billion takeover by Nubank broke down and discussions with buyout firms over a minority stake stalled. The Financial Times has [more here](https://www.ft.com/content/6553ec40-00a8-4832-8ca5-c528b9da224a).

**Thinking Machines Lab**, the nearly two-year-old San Francisco startup co-founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, is reportedly raising a $5 billion round at a $40 billion pre-money valuation led by previous investor **Accel**. The company lets businesses train and customize AI models on their own data. Business Insider first reported the news, and Tech Funding News has [more here](https://techfundingnews.com/report-accel-is-to-lead-a-5b-for-thinking-machines-lab-at-a-40b-valuation/).
