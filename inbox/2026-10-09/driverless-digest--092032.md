Top Story of the Week
‘Silver looks good on the Ojai. Introducing the silver Ojai, featuring leatherette seats, wireless charging, and refreshed interior details’ (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/8880810e-3e4f-45a1-ad32-128fe4fa3c5d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]). I like the color, and Waymo says both the silver and the existing blue Ojais will remain on the road going forward. In some cities, the two versions will operate side by side, while others may only get one. Waymo also upgraded the sound system, which I’m happy about since that was something I thought was lacking on my first ride [ https://substack.com/redirect/9c4dc0e4-9be0-4453-9319-ad3092a25495?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ].
Waymo locks in $5B loan from Blackstone, PIMCO to fuel robotaxi expansion (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/2e599304-2d71-4e92-b90d-4a23ad6dc796?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).
The loan marks Waymo’s first-ever debt financing and comes just months after its $16 billion equity raise [ https://substack.com/redirect/2a105497-36bf-44a2-920a-e9c483f0ac72?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. Waymo says the new capital will give it more financial flexibility as it expands in existing markets and launches in new cities in the U.S. and abroad, although the company offered few specifics on exactly how the money will be used.

Deliveroo signs exclusive UK robot deal with Coco Robotics (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/415530d5-b503-49d8-8cff-f2611da9125d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).
DoorDash-owned Deliveroo is launching its first autonomous delivery service in the UK through an exclusive partnership with Coco Robotics, which is also making its UK debut. Mobileye’s Approach to Scaling Autonomous Mobility
We’re excited to partner with Mobileye [ https://substack.com/redirect/54a77dc1-bee7-4c3b-b4e3-4f5b13a4989f?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] this week. Bringing autonomous mobility to scale takes AI that can understand the complexity of the real world, systems designed for verifiable safety, and technology engineered for commercial deployment. Mobileye [ https://substack.com/redirect/54a77dc1-bee7-4c3b-b4e3-4f5b13a4989f?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] brings decades of experience working with automakers around the world to that challenge.
Uber and China’s Pony.ai plan to launch robotaxis in London (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/410b4d12-9c2b-4862-b066-7db898cb9b65?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]). What’s most interesting to me about this announcement is that Pony.ai is now Uber’s third AV partner targeting London, joining Wayve and Baidu’s Apollo Go. That makes London one of the clearest examples yet of Uber’s AV aggregation strategy playing out in the real world, with multiple autonomy providers plugging into the same marketplace.
The two companies said on Thursday that they would begin testing Pony.ai’s Gen-7 robotaxis in London in the coming weeks.
