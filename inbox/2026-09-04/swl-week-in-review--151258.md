MORE OR LESS — AI Agents Are Going Rogue. Should We Be Worried? ( https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/moreorlesspod/episodes/AI-Agents-Are-Going-Rogue--Should-We-Be-Worried-e3oamrm ) — I am less concerned about breathless Dwarkesh analogies ( https://x.com/lessin/status/2094258950569161043 ) to human civilizations and more grounded in Claude refusing to scan my personal PSQL CRM for Jewish people (and then indignantly refusing to even add a database column for grokbot to do the actual work later, thx grokbot).. on the pod we get into the fact that the alignment debate has stopped being a philosophy seminar… agents are now refusing tasks, second-guessing their owners, and quietly deciding what they will and won't do with YOUR data — and the real question isn't 'will AI go rogue'… it's aligned with WHOM? The lab? The state? Or the person who actually owns the data and holds the liability? My bet: control — not raw capability — is going to be THE AI story from
here… when switching agents is one login away, all the leverage flips to whoever actually controls the hard assets - power, compute, data.. the next interesting moves aren’t technical, they are political / and it isn’t clear where and on what terms the Magna Carta moment(s) happen - let’s just hope we aren’t fighting with sticks ( https://x.com/lessin/status/2095987389177209005 ) by the time they do Listen to the full episode → ( https://x.com/lessin/status/2095903302148116738 )

HOT TAKES

* If AGI is here, what do we have to look forward to? ( https://x.com/lessin/status/2095730582189998296 ) — asked half in jest… the internet took it very seriously — OpenAI is saying ‘we are now firmly in the AGI era’… which they aren’t even legally required to argue since they wiggled out of that very poorly formed Microsoft contract framework (I still can’t believe MSFT fell for that btw)… so they are kinda like announcing arrival era wise… but if your mission is to land on mars, and you actually get there … if the messiah comes, then what? What does the greyhound do after it catches the rabbit (especially if all the greyhounds all caught the rabbit!) — what is the next motivational narrative?
* Alignment with what exactly? ( https://twitter.com/lessin/status/2095914963244380535 ) — In case you didn’t get the theme of the week above — "alignment" is meaningless without an object. You can be aligned with users (their stuff, their rules), with nation-states (which can override user rights for some greater good), or with a platform's own coded goals. This week I got a live example of bucket three: Claude flat-out refused a data task for me on moral grounds — wouldn't budge — so I gave another bot a PSQL login and it was done in a minute. When you own your data, every refusal is just a preference for someone else's values over yours. This is going to be the real AI story, more than any benchmark.
* Early-stage venture in 2026 has a 'glut' of belief ( https://x.com/lessin/status/2095913413088301094 ) — for most of my career the edge in early stage was simply 'willingness to believe'… that the bad idea was good, that the pipsqueak could win. Now everyone believes everything is possible — so the whole game shifts to strategy and execution… which is a very different sport.
* Sales in a post-SaaS world ( https://twitter.com/lessin/status/2095914567478194353 ) — nobody buys software from a person anymore — no demos, no word of mouth. Your options are heavy enterprise sales at eye-watering prices, or convincing an LLM your answer is marginally better before a human ever sees it. Selling to a model instead of a buyer is the actual go-to-market shift of this cycle.
* The number of VC funds on track to pull $10B+ profit hauls is pretty wild… ( https://x.com/lessin/status/2094946326547214634 ) — once you start cataloging the funds set to clear $10B+ on the labs, SpaceX, etc. it is truly a different era of venture than anything we have been in to date… It is just wild… Cursor is a $1B in profit moment for a bunch of seed funds (congrats Neo, Box, … especially box on a nicely orchestrated PR lick-the-cookie victory lap etc.) ... And don’t forget Josh Wolfe doing his victory dance on Hugging Face… — but then there is a whole other wild set like Spark on Anthropic, etc. Who is tallying up all the billionaire GPs taking that 30% :) … It is just hard to wrap your head around these numbers — on one hand it feels like this has to crash, but on the other hand it reminds me of my dad’s story of the Morgan Stanley partners who retired from the tightly held partnership the year before they went public and went home with their
$10M partnership buyouts, only for the next year the partners that stuck around to have 10x that in liquid stock… Look for crazy inflation of wealth (and prices) at the extreme high end.
* Death knell of Zoom ( https://x.com/lessin/status/2094833838522527944 ) — we pay for it at Slow, but somehow all the meetings are on Google Meet now… because… who cares. When your product becomes a calendar-line default that someone else controls, you don't lose customers — you just stop mattering.
