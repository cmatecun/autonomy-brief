Erebor Passes $7B in Deposits
Exciting news from the Financial Times [ https://substack.com/redirect/9add98a8-4e1a-452a-910b-88b5b04855d9?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] today for Palmer Luckey’s Erebor. The bank just passed $7B in deposits just eight months after launching back in February. Over the last quarter, they’ve added more than 500 customers and $3B+ in deposits, according to sources close to FT. Remember, it was just a couple months ago when Erebor announced $4B of deposits [ https://substack.com/redirect/15451427-f506-4168-b192-da08eaad094d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ].
What’s interesting is how little of that money Erebor is actually lending out so far. At the end of Q2 Erebor made around $78M in loans [ https://substack.com/redirect/f2bf19df-ea23-4979-b0df-30aafc9a9242?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] and was keeping about 90% of its assets in cash at other banks.
Whenever there’s news around Erebor, I’m reminded of Palmer’s appearance on the show earlier this year, because I think it gets at what the bank is actually trying to be.
“Erebor is a bank for Americans”, “It’s a bank for startups”, “It’s a bank for the crypto industry.”
I think Palmer puts all those pieces into a much cleaner framework here:
Think of it as the opposite of HSBC. They were banking the cartels, desperately trying to avoid government interventions that would make that clear to the public markets. Put Erebor at the other end of that spectrum.
There are a lot of banks that — whether they are good or bad people is irrelevant — you might have good people who nonetheless are very beholden to European markets, Chinese markets, other foreign markets that they need to keep happy. Either because they want to keep them happy to make money, or because they don’t want their executives in those jurisdictions to get arrested.
Erebor is taking a very different approach. We’re an American company, we’re an American bank that supports American companies, and we will comply with US law, but we will not comply with spurious rulings from people who have no real jurisdiction or authority over us. And you’ll have a very hard time finding a bank that takes that position. I’m not aware of any other than us.
