Top Story of the Week
Uber Starts Offering Amazon’s Driverless Zoox Rides in Las Vegas (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/4e3c5a73-8a6b-4afe-905c-08a0f4cac825?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], no paywall [ https://substack.com/redirect/8e5f3e30-618d-4e28-9df3-bd1812a4721c?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).
Riders requesting a regular Uber ride may be matched with a Zoox vehicle at no additional cost, the companies said Monday. New Orleans, Jefferson Parish collaborate to write robotaxi rules before Waymo goes driverless (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/947facc4-d619-460f-ac45-3579d06d1849?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).
The proposed rules would treat for-hire autonomous vehicles as taxicab companies. If enacted, they would require registration and per-ride fees, mandate insurance coverage and on-call support, provide a way for public safety personnel to disable the vehicles and forbid them from idling in residential areas or bike lanes.
