# Anthropic Delivers a Monster IPO

**From:** StrictlyVC <connie@strictlyvc.com>  
**Date:** 2026-11-20T06:45:23.000Z

## Top News

**Anthropic**’s shares opened at $111.50, 34% above their $83 IPO price, briefly pushing the Claude maker’s **market value** to **$2 trillion** and making it the first publicly traded frontier AI company, surpassing SpaceX’s June debut. Reuters has [more here](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/anthropics-historic-debut-put-ais-promise-peril-wall-st-test-2026-11-19/).

**Anthropic**’s IPO could ultimately direct roughly **$156 billion to philanthropy**, including about $80 billion through its Public Benefit Fund and an estimated $76 billion from employee pledges and company matches, though the final amount will depend on employees following through and Anthropic’s stock price. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-ipo-could-deliver-156-billion-to-charity/).

**Anthropic**’s IPO is delivering **enormous returns** to venture investors that were willing to write unusually large checks, including **Spark Capital,** which led a $450 million round in 2023 at a $4.5 billion valuation, and **Menlo Ventures**, whose $1+ billion investment for a roughly 1.5% stake is now worth over $20 billion. Reuters has [more here](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/anthropics-15-trillion-debut-rewrites-venture-capitals-playbook-2026-11-19/).

## Anthropic’s 7 Founders Cashed Out Nearly $2B in Record-Breaking IPO

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/29f43575-ddb2-4396-808d-fbf260d08637/antrhopic-openingday-getty.jpeg?t=1795153755)
Caption: Image Credits: Charly Triballeau / AFP / Getty Images

**By Julie Bort**

Anthropic became a public company on Thursday, raising a total of $81.3 billion, including $1.95 billion for its seven founders. As we previously reported, each of them [_ponied up about 3.4 million shares apiece_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/all-7-anthropic-founders-will-each-cash-out-about-250m-in-ipo/) to sell in the IPO.

At the upgraded IPO price of $83 per share, that means they each pocketed about $280 million before underwriting fees and taxes. It looked like they had calculated the number of shares they planned to sell to give them each around $250 million if shares hit $74. That was the midpoint price from Anthropic’s original price range. So that’s a cool $30-ish million more for each of them than perhaps they were gunning for.

Anthropic implied in its IPO filings that the founders aren’t entirely in this for the money though. The company said that each of the founders has pledged to dedicate 80% of their personal equity in the company [_to philanthropy._](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-ipo-could-deliver-156-billion-to-charity/)

However, if any of them struggle to live on their quarter of a billion, they can scale back their charitable commitments. The company points out that these commitments are “voluntary, revocable expressions of each co-founder’s philanthropic intent and do not constitute legally binding obligations.”

With the IPO in the record books, there were a few other interesting numbers worth highlighting.

**$104.8 billion.** That’s really the amount Anthropic raised for its coffers (with a possibility of $117 billion, if the bankers buy all of their allotment of shares). In addition to selling its Class A common stock, it also sold a previously announced $26 billion worth of a special non-voting Class N stock to some of the biggest tech names in the industry.

Read more (https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-7-founders-cashed-out-nearly-2b-in-record-breaking-ipo/)

## Massive Fundings

**Ansa Biotechnologies**, an eight-year-old startup based in Emeryville, CA, that produces long, complex synthetic DNA for drug discovery, vaccines, cell and gene therapies, and biological research, raised a $71.5 million Series C round led by **Blue Water Life Science Advisors**, with **Illumina Ventures**, **BEVC**, **Lightscape Partners**, and **Agent Capital** as well as previous investors **Cerberus Ventures**, **Fall Line Capital**, **Dauntless Ventures**, and **Altitude Life Science Ventures** also stepping up. The company has raised a total of $211 million. [_More here_](https://pulse2.com/ansa-biotechnologies-raises-71-5-million-to-scale-dna-synthesis/).

**Boltz**, a one-year-old London startup that develops AI models that help drug researchers design molecules and select candidates for laboratory testing, raised a $110 million Series A round led by **Bessemer Venture Partners**, with **Benchling** as well as previous investors **Andreessen Horowitz**, **Amplify Partners**, **Zetta Venture Partners**, **Obvious Ventures**, **Greycroft**, **F-Prime Capital**, and **Hummingbird** also taking part. The company has raised a total of $138 million. [_More here_](https://www.finsmes.com/2026/11/boltz-raises-110m-in-series-a-funding.html).

**Linq**, a seven-year-old San Francisco startup that provides messaging, voice, and computer-use infrastructure that lets AI agents communicate, operate software, and complete tasks, raised a $90 million Series B round led by **Andreessen Horowitz**, with additional support from **Anthos** and **Left Lane Capital** as well as previous investors **TQ Ventures**, **Mucker Capital**, and **Abstract**. The SaaS News has [_more here_](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/linq-raises-90m-series-b/).

**MegaRobo**, a 10-year-old Beijing company that builds robotic systems for AI-driven scientific experiments and precision manufacturing, raised a $200 million round. Investors included **Oman Investment Authority**, **Asia Investment Capital**, **Granite Asia**, **Sinovation Ventures**, and **CMB International**. The company has raised a total of $600 million. SiliconANGLE has [more here](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/19/china-based-ai-robotics-biotech-firm-megarobo-raises-200m-to-accelerate-scientific-discovery/).

## Big-But-Not-Crazy-Big Fundings

**Everful**, a two-year-old San Francisco and Singapore startup that connects smaller retailers and brands with Chinese factories and manages sourcing, quality control, and logistics, raised a $48.5 million Series A co-led by **Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India** and **January Capital**, with previous investors **Square Peg** and **Lightspeed** also stepping up. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/former-alibaba-execs-everful-raises-48-5m-to-connect-retailers-with-chinese-factories/).

**Nodal Networks**, a two-year-old Los Angeles startup that develops AI tools that help container terminals predict congestion and plan container placement, equipment allocation, and loading, raised an $11.3 million seed round led by **Eniac Ventures**, with **NewRoad Capital Partners** as well as previous investors **Riot Ventures**, **Trail Mix Ventures**, and **Liquid 2 Ventures** also participating. The company has raised a total of $14.5 million. SiliconANGLE has [_more here_](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/19/nodal-networks-raises-11-3m-to-solve-global-shipping-bottlenecks/).

**StepSecurity**, a five-year-old Seattle startup that detects and blocks malicious code across developer machines, code repositories, package registries, and software deployment workflows, raised a $20 million Series A round led by **Elephant**, with previous investors **Runtime Ventures**, **Inner Loop Capital**, and **SaaS Ventures** also anteing up. The company has raised a total of $23 million. [_More here_](https://fintech.global/2026/11/19/stepsecurity-bags-20m-as-supply-chain-attacks-escalate/).

## Smaller Fundings

**LATO**, a London startup founded this year that uses AI agents to interview customers and industry experts for investment firms conducting commercial due diligence, raised a $4.7 million seed round co-led by **Air Street Capital** and **Axiom Partners**, with additional participation from **Y Combinator**, **Montis VC**, **Inception Fund**, and **Paul Graham**. Tech Funding News has [_more here_](https://techfundingnews.com/yc-backed-lato-raises-4-7m-seed-to-put-ai-agents-on-private-equitys-due-diligence-calls/).

**Tesorai**, a two-year-old San Diego startup that uses AI to reprocess raw life sciences data so researchers can search and compare findings across experiments, raised a $5.3 million seed round. Investors included **Perplexity Fund**, **IQT**, **South Central Ventures**, **New Stack Ventures**, **NextGen Venture Partners**, **f7 Ventures**, **VitalStage Ventures**, and **ZAKA**. The company has raised a total of $8 million. Tech Funding News has [_more here_](https://techfundingnews.com/tesorai-raises-5-3m-seed-backed-by-perplexity-fund-and-iqt-to-make-raw-lab-data-searchable/).

**Wilgot**, a two-year-old Stockholm startup that rewrites brands’ product pages, feeds, and FAQs so AI shopping assistants can understand and recommend their products, raised a $3 million seed round led by **Industrifonden**, with **Luminar Ventures**, **Foundry Ventures**, **Ventures Together**, and **Inovexus** also chiming in. [Tech.eu](https://Tech.eu) has [_more here_](https://tech.eu/2026/11/19/wilgot-raises-3m-in-seed-funding-to-help-brands-get-recommended-by-ai/).

## Going Public

More than half of the roughly 1,200 institutional investors that sought shares in **Anthropic**’s blockbuster IPO received **no allocation**, with shares heavily skewed toward U.S.-based buyers and many overseas orders shut out entirely. Bloomberg has [more here](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/anthropic-ipo-said-to-give-nothing-to-most-institutional-buyers).

**Anthropic**’s IPO will make it by far the **largest** **publicly traded public benefit corporation**, testing an unusual governance structure that requires the company to balance shareholder returns with its mission to develop AI safely, while its seven founders retain 50.1% of voting control and an independent trust can appoint several board members. The New York Times has [more here](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/18/business/anthropic-ipo-corporate-governance.html).

## People

Anthropic CEO **Dario Amodei** used the company’s Nasdaq debut to pledge that Anthropic will pace the training and deployment of AI systems for as long as necessary to ensure they are safe and remain under human control, saying it will not bend to business or political pressure. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/at-nasdaq-opening-bell-anthropic-ceo-recommits-to-pacing-ai-development/).

Facebook co-founder **Dustin Moskovitz**, one of Anthropic’s earliest investors, argues that “A.I. is moving too fast” and says frontier labs should stop racing, even as his early stake became worth billions. He transferred the shares to a non-profit in 2025 so he and his wife would not personally profit from the IPO. The New York Times has [more here](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/19/business/anthropic-ipo-dustin-moskovitz.html).

**Elon Musk** says SpaceX has completed the design of miniature rooftop cell towers meant to eliminate indoor mobile dead zones that Starlink satellites can’t reach, with production expected in 2027 alongside a new Direct-to-Cell satellite network designed to increase bandwidth 40-fold. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/elon-musk-says-spacex-has-a-plan-to-eliminate-cell-phone-dead-zones/).

## Essential Reads

**Meta**’s fast-growing **Muse AI agent**, downloaded more than 10 million times since September, has repeatedly accessed private messages, fabricated purchase and financial information, and sent users chats that appeared to belong to other people, prompting internal concerns about potential **cross-account data leaks**. Reuters has [more here](https://www.reuters.com/investigations/meta-bets-big-muse-ai-agent-early-safety-mishaps-surface-2026-11-18/).

After speaking with dozens of current and former **Anthropic** employees, New York Times Opinion columnist Jasmine Sun describes a company increasingly consumed by the fear that **AI development** is moving too fast even as it plans to use its IPO proceeds to buy more computing power and reach recursively self-improving AI before its rivals. [More here](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/18/opinion/anthropic-ai-openai.html).

North American companies ordered nearly 11,000 **industrial robots** in the third quarter, up 25% from a year earlier, as manufacturers turn to automation to address labor shortages; orders for collaborative robots jumped 56%. The Financial Times has [more here](https://www.ft.com/content/747f5533-1bab-4143-a78b-ed4e2b575340).

A 70-year-old participant in a Caltech-USC brain-computer-interface study is **fighting for access to neural data** generated by six chips implanted in his brain after researchers told him the information belonged to them, highlighting the **absence of clear U.S. rules **governing ownership of brain data. The New York Times has [more here](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/17/business/brain-data-access-rights-buckwalter.html).
