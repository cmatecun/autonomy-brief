🎮 We’re live with the CEO behind GTA VI
Massive day on the timeline with both GTA VI dropping and Anthropic’s IPO.
We’re kicking the day off by interviewing Strauss Zelnick, the CEO behind GTA VI (he’s live right now) and General Catalyst Hemant Taneja to discuss the Anthropic IPO.
GTA VI Is Here
After 13 years of waiting, GTA VI is finally out and it lived up to the hype. According to Bloomberg [ https://substack.com/redirect/5b4c8d62-98e8-421e-8e40-779681ec5e45?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], it’s the best-reviewed video game of all time with a 99/100 on Metacritic, 98/100 on OpenCritic, and a perfect 10/10 on IGN and GameSpot. Bloomberg’s video game reporter Jason Schreier [ https://substack.com/redirect/539efe35-369c-4969-a250-c7a821e329f2?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] said it was one of the greatest video games he’s ever played.
Head of Gusto Insights and Senior Economist Nich Tremper wrote a piece [ https://substack.com/redirect/3382b4bd-bdde-4301-9a0d-0d4cd0ab2a7d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] showing that GTA VI’s launch is apparently big enough to show up in payroll data. The amount of workers calling out today is 9% higher than on the previous two Thursdays.
Also check out John’s deep dive on YouTube into The Business of Grand Theft Auto.
Anthropic’s IPO
Anthropic officially [ https://substack.com/redirect/32181b7f-ab12-430d-b269-768d5d887db5?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] went public today in the biggest IPO ever, raising $81.3B at a $1.5T valuation. Shares opened at $111.50, 34% above the $83 IPO price, briefly valuing the company around $2T. As of 10:30am PT the stock price sits around $95/share.
We’ll be discussing on the show today who are the biggest winners from the IPO and what’s in store for Anthropic.
