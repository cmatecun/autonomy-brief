## Sequoia Hits Rewind, Undoing 20 Years of Expansion

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/b3d877f1-6cd1-4fd2-8a71-ad6432d6c621/Screen-Shot-2023-01-13-at-3.33.43-PM__1_.jpeg?t=1793840572)
Caption: Image Credits: Dani Padgett / StrictlyVC

**By Connie Loizos**

Sequoia Capital posted a [_memo to X_](https://x.com/sequoia/status/2117961312143855764) on Wednesday morning titled “The Sequoia of Tomorrow” that’s full of surprises. The firm has raised roughly $10 billion in new funds, it says. It is also closing its London office, shutting down its Arc accelerator and its Scouts program, merging its early-stage and growth teams into one, flattening its partnership, and killing the Monday partner meeting.

The framing is forward-looking, but the messaging is very much about rewinding to an earlier time when the team was smaller and focused more narrowly on Silicon Valley.

Sequoia says as much itself. The firm describes coming out of a roughly 20-year expansionary phase during which it built multiple businesses in multiple geographies and says an unintended side effect was hierarchy. Nearly everything being undone here was built during that stretch, under a succession of stewards running from Doug Leone to Jim Goetz to Roelof Botha.

One of the most surprising developments is that the firm is killing two programs that put Sequoia closest to young founders. [_Scouts_](https://techcrunch.com/2019/06/07/a-peek-inside-sequoia-capitals-low-flying-wide-reaching-scout-program/), which Sequoia launched around 2009 and, anecdotally, half the industry has since copied in some form, gave small checks to founders and operators to invest on the firm’s behalf; it was a way to see deals through people closer to interesting new founders than any partner could be. Jason Calacanis famously pointed Sequoia to Uber through the program. Sam Altman brought Stripe to Sequoia as a scout, too. Other early scouts included Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky and Dropbox founders Arash Ferdowsi and Drew Houston.

[_Arc_](https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/07/sequoia-wants-to-invest-1-million-in-your-idea-then-teach-you-how-to-really-sell-it/), launched in March 2022, was Sequoia’s answer to Y Combinator: a batch accelerator for pre-seed and seed founders, running several cohorts a year for seven weeks, with admitted companies getting $500,000 to $1 million up front.

Both were bets on being early and being everywhere. Now Sequoia says it wants to put more energy behind fewer people and things.

Read more (https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/sequoia-hits-rewind-undoing-20-years-of-expansion/)

## Massive Fundings

**EloElo**, a six-year-old Bengaluru startup that streams serialized short-form dramas in six languages through its Story TV mobile app, raised an $80 million Series C round at a $500 million valuation. The deal was co-led by **Hornbill Capital** and **NBCUniversal**, with **MIT Investment Co** as well as previous investors **Play Ventures**, **Kalaari Capital**, **WaterBridge Ventures**, and **Griffin Gaming Partners** also stepping up. The company has raised a total of $130 million. TechCrunch has [_more here_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/indias-eloelo-nabs-80m-from-nbc-universal-to-bet-on-the-countrys-rising-microdrama-market).

**Nooks**, a five-year-old San Francisco startup that uses AI agents to automate sales work from prospecting through renewals, secured a $500 million growth financing commitment from **General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund**, a non-equity vehicle that takes a share of the returns generated by the capital it provides. Reuters has [more here](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/ai-sales-startup-nooks-secures-500-million-funding-expansion-2026-11-04/).

**Pliant**, a six-year-old Berlin fintech that provides commercial cards, payment accounts, foreign exchange, and spend-management infrastructure for businesses, raised an $80 million Series C co-led by **Seaya Nova** and **Speedinvest**, with **Illuminate Financial**, **Motive Partners**, and **Molten Ventures** also buying in. The Next Web has [more here](https://thenextweb.com/news/pliant-raises-80m-to-open-its-b2b-payments-platform-to-ai-agents).

**Revolut**, an 11-year-old London company that provides digital banking and money-management services to retail customers, completed a secondary share sale at a $115 billion valuation, up from the $75 billion valuation it secured last November. The deal was co-led by **Glade Brook Capital Partners**, **Coatue**, **Durable Capital Partners**, **T. Rowe Price**, and **Baillie Gifford**. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/revoluts-valuation-jumps-53-to-115b-in-new-share-sale-as-it-prepares-to-launch-us-bank/).

**Sentra**, a five-year-old New York startup that identifies sensitive corporate data and governs which employees, systems, and AI agents can access it, raised a $150 million Series C round led by **Capital One Ventures**, with previous investors **Bessemer Venture Partners**, **Zeev Ventures**, **Standard Investments**, **Munich Re Ventures**, and **Key1 Capital** also chipping in. The deal included a small secondary component for employees and brought the company's total funding to $250+ million. CTech has [_more here_](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/s1jextotml).

**Traversal**, a three-year-old New York startup that uses AI agents to diagnose, fix, and prevent software infrastructure failures for enterprise engineering teams, raised a $120 million round at a $1 billion post-money valuation. The deal was co-led by **Redpoint Ventures** and **Khosla Ventures** as well as previous investor **Kleiner Perkins**, with **Battery Ventures** and **Amex Ventures** as well as **prior backer Sequoia Capital** also buying in. The company has raised a total of $173 million. SiliconANGLE has [_more here_](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/03/autonomous-site-reliability-engineering-startup-traversal-bags-120m-in-funding-at-a-1b-valuation/).

The company has raised a total of $110 million. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/titan-os-raises-46-million-to-expand-its-smart-tv-os-internationally/).
