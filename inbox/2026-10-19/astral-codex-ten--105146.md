This is the weekly visible open thread. Post about anything you want, ask random questions, whatever. ACX has an unofficial subreddit [ https://substack.com/redirect/380c85f7-8533-4f86-b238-62878832bb51?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], Discord [ https://substack.com/redirect/1ab6f5ea-580d-4d07-a4e8-8fe3f9ba0897?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], and bulletin board [ https://substack.com/redirect/e0ee884c-a561-4aba-ab17-e83b2a1c434d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], and in-person meetups around the world [ https://substack.com/redirect/8c4f1052-789e-4a7c-836f-4a53ef777350?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. Most content is free, some is subscriber only; you can subscribe here [ https://substack.com/redirect/e94fcebb-2366-4a3e-b814-01dbf793e98d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. Also:
1: Meetup changes in Rockville, Northampton, Eindhoven, Great Barrington, Cairo, Portland, and Sofia. Check the post [ https://substack.com/redirect/f507b7b1-6301-4da4-b0f5-2f2ae6bc13f9?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] for more information.
2: The Boston ACX meetup group has finished their Greater Boston general election voting guide [ https://substack.com/redirect/af93e586-9f17-4782-8a92-9e5c56ce9920?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. I may have more of these in the upcoming week. Still trying to figure out if we can do something for the Bay Area, but remember that SF mail-in ballot registration due date is end-of-day today (Monday), and that our main SF endorsement is Scott Wiener for Congress. We may have more later.
3: I’m continuing to work on modeling (and maybe one day automating) some parts of AI grantmaking (you can see previous efforts here [ https://substack.com/redirect/eefa7a01-f16c-4f06-8184-99e2e7a3ebf3?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]). I would once again like people who are well-versed in the AI/grantmaking worlds to take a survey on how much they value various things. This one involves assigning utility weights to ~80 different AI safety accomplishments. Take it here [ https://substack.com/redirect/332df5ea-234b-421a-9170-884dab6e43f1?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. After you’ve taken that survey, if you still have more time/energy it would be extra helpful for my data collection if you would take this alternate version [ https://substack.com/redirect/9b4e17da-6bbf-46cd-86a3-6fa7bcaac4f3?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], which asks you to predict how I would answer the first survey (so I can separate out worldview differences from weight-choosing random noise).
4: Bay Area ACX meetup is scheduled for this coming Saturday, 10/24, 1 PM, at Mox in SF (1680 Mission St). I’ll probably make a post about it, but consider this an early heads-up.
5: Grantee and Manifold Markets co-founder Stephen Grugett announces his latest project: MNX [ https://substack.com/redirect/6aaf0aa5-5e75-43a9-a9c1-4ceaefb6c0cc?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] is a real-money prediction market on AI. It lets people sell futures representing AI company stocks, bet on AI company revenues directly, and a few other things like Epoch Capabilities Index targets. Interestingly, despite the talk of Anthropic’s API falling from from $2T to $1.5T, both Polymarket and MNX still price it around $2T.
6: Friend of the blog Goodheart Labs is working on Common Notes, aka “Twitter Community Notes, but for everything”. They’re implementing it through a combination of AIs (who write most notes), users (who write others, and vote on which ones are helpful), and a browser extension (which makes the system show up on everything, including this blog). Check it out, and install the browser extension,  here. [ https://substack.com/redirect/40745ffd-306d-4e96-87f9-98665c2cb0cf?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]
