Crypto Is Going “Bunker Mode”
Ethereum founder Vitalik Buterin and many others in crypto are sounding the alarm that AI-driven math advances could threaten blockchain security [ https://substack.com/redirect/6ce4a23a-b39f-4aea-9f5b-3eda571bdcb8?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ].
Matthew Green, who teaches cryptography at Johns Hopkins, put the situation in stark relief: “I think we might lose public key cryptography.”
At the moment, cryptography as a discipline has been exempted from the list of published papers related to AI-enabled math breakthroughs, but everyone suspects that this is purely by choice, and by no means a function of some key model limitation.
Much like the broader cybersecurity scare that happened back in March of this year, there is fortunately a gap between open-source models catch up on capability, but the 6 month delay estimate seems continually to be a useful guide. Anthropic announced Mythos Preview on April 7, 2026, and exactly 6 months later, CrowdStrike announced [ https://substack.com/redirect/acba8b66-c80d-4d96-8150-c1690278fa3f?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] that a 26-year-old used a Chinese-developed AI agent to execute cyberattacks against South Korean banks.
So as the crypto community goes “bunker mode” I’m sure an incredible amount of progress will be made on “AI proofing systems” and the market seems to believe that it’s not all doom and gloom. Bitcoin is only down ~3% today and still up ~3% for the month. Still, lots to do if you work in crypto, or potentially even if you only hold crypto, so be careful out there.
