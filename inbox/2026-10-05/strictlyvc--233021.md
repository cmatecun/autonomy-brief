# The Pentagon Stops Using Claude

**From:** StrictlyVC <connie@strictlyvc.com>  
**Date:** 2026-10-06T06:30:21.000Z

---

## Top News

The **Pentagon** says it has stopped using **Anthropic**'s Claude after the company refused to remove safeguards against mass surveillance and fully autonomous weapons. The military had relied on Claude for months for classified research, intelligence analysis and operations, including through **Palantir**'s Maven system. BBC has [more here](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5j9x9pr0240o).

**Vinod Khosla** called the AI coding startup **Factory **a "struggling second tier competitor" last week, and **Menlo Ventures** answered today with a glowing blog post, touting its investment with the company. A source tells TechCrunch the firm would have invested even more in Factory’s last round if it could have. [More here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/after-factorys-public-spat-with-khosla-menlo-proudly-invests/).

**Hackers** stole personal information belonging to roughly **8 million Danish citizens** and residents from a government identity database, an extraordinary breach in a country with an estimated population of just 6 million; the compromised data included names, addresses, social security numbers, and other information. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/hackers-steal-8-million-citizens-records-from-danish-government-database/).

## Etched Fields Funding Offers at $40B+ Valuation, Sources Say

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/0a8cdd9a-9e36-4154-be62-1e7977c83627/Sequoia_Etched-Abhishek-Malani-Robert-Wachen-Sonya-Huang-Gavin-Uberti-copy.jpeg?t=1791259946)
Caption: Image Credits: Etched

**By Marina Temkin**

Although it’s only been a couple of months since Etched raised [$700 million at a $21 billion valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/etcheds-valuation-doubles-to-21b-in-a-month/), the AI chip startup is already being plied with investment offers at double or more its value, according to people familiar with the company.

[Etched](https://www.etched.com/) is reviewing incoming bids that range from $40 billion from top-tier investors to $50 billion from lesser-known backers, one person said. These fundraising talks are early, so terms of any deal, should one happen, may change. Etched declined to comment.

While this may seem like a fast timetable to raise another mega round, Etched is pursuing a particularly expensive segment of the AI industry: building full AI hardware systems powered by its own proprietary chips. The person familiar with these offers said that if it raises as much as its last round, this could give Etched a cushion of as much as 3.5 years of runway.

There are reasons why VCs are hot to own a piece of Etched. The four-year-old startup shows promise of challenging Nvidia. Not only did quant trading firm Jane Street lead the last $700 million round, it is also a customer that took delivery of an early system. Etched said in July that it had already secured $1 billion in customer orders, including the one from Jane Street, after manufacturing its test chip at a TSMC factory this summer.

Co-founder and COO Robert Wachen previously told TechCrunch that investors are so enthusiastic because Etched has designed [two new components from scratch to speed up inference](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/etcheds-valuation-doubles-to-21b-in-a-month/) — the computing process that happens after a user submits a prompt.

## Essential Reads

New York Magazine interviews defectors from **OpenAI**, **Anthropic**, and **Google DeepMind** who left over fears that the race toward **increasingly autonomous AI **is moving faster than companies can control it, with several describing the powerful psychological pull of staying inside the labs even when they believe the work could have catastrophic consequences. New York Magazine has [more here](https://nymag.com/intelligencer/article/ai-researchers-quit-openai-anthropic.html).

Businesses are **struggling to budget for AI **because token usage can vary wildly even for the same task: only 11% of nearly 400 companies surveyed accurately forecast their AI spending, while researchers found that lower-priced models actually cost more than pricier ones in 32% of tests. The Wall Street Journal has [more here](https://www.wsj.com/tech/personal-tech/ai-token-spending-businesses-431ee94a).

Two former **Groq** engineers sued over **Nvidia’s** $20 billion licensing-and-hiring deal with the AI chip startup, alleging Groq’s board bypassed a required shareholder vote, accepted a “lowball” price, and structured the transaction to benefit insiders while leaving other stockholders shortchanged. CNBC has [more here](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/05/nvidia-groq-deal-stockholder-lawsuit.html).

Independent researchers are tracking a **“fleet” of AI agents** apparently running on Tencent infrastructure and querying Alibaba’s Amap service for directions to entrances at public places, activity they say involves many parallel agents performing similar tasks without evidence of coordination. TechCrunch has [more here](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/researchers-are-tracking-a-chinese-ai-agent-fleet/).
