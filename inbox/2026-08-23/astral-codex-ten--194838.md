1: We’re still looking for EA college meetup organizers [ https://substack.com/redirect/39852b6c-308d-47c9-b589-fdfce64c660d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. 2: ACX’s Boston meetup group has finished their voting guide  [ https://substack.com/redirect/bfc11686-daf2-44bb-8bdf-68c221cb90d0?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]for the Greater Boston primary election (early voting in August, election day on September 1). I’m still excited about signal-boosting voting guides from meetup groups in big cities.
3: Jacob Trefethen is looking to hire grantmakers [ https://substack.com/@jacobtrefethen/note/c-319184875 ] for the OpenAI Foundation, and says the roles “may be a good fit for Substackers or Substack lurkers”. He writes:
Here are a few of the program roles:
Head of Econ Lab, to lead a team figuring out the economics of transformative AI
Program Director, Formal Methods, to support verifiable defense systems as cybersecurity dynamics change
Program Officer/Director on High-Burden Diseases, helping channel R&D to fight the diseases most plaguing humanity
Program Officer/Director on Catalytic Deployment, figuring out how best to apply AI in the social sector
…and more than a dozen others. Please share with friends who you think may be interested! […] Salary ranges are posted on the job descriptions [ https://substack.com/redirect/89541d18-f04e-4339-abe0-a3f499e3cb7d?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ].
I think these getting the best people in these positions would be good even if you otherwise oppose OpenAI or AI in general; if you disagree with me about this, send me an email arguing why and maybe I’ll avoid boosting the next one.
