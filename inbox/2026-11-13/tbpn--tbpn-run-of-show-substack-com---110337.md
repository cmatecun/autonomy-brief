Everything We’re Following with NVIDIA Today
It seems like the dust is starting to settle around the Jensen/NVIDIA news. Kind of hard to follow with every media outlet having their take on the news. Here are the headlines and stories we’re still following and will be commenting on the show today:
Before His Ouster, Jensen Huang Belittled the Executive Replacing Him – WSJ [ https://substack.com/redirect/842a58d0-23bd-43d7-9578-06c6f04e171b?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]
The Wall Street Journal reports that now interim CEO Colette Kress pushed back on some of Jensen’s more aggressive AI deals before taking over. In an August meeting, Jensen reportedly called her a “deer in headlights.” The Journal writes, “Kress was a ‘reporting CFO,’ not someone who could help him define a strategy or make decisions, Huang said in a meeting.”
Nvidia Employees Petition for Jensen Huang’s Return – WIRED [ https://substack.com/redirect/9b3a9511-486b-4cfe-b4aa-edfdb348c97e?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]
WIRED reports that an internal post about Jensen’s future at NVIDIA has over 1,600 comments from employees, with the majority pushing for him to return as CEO.
‘I’m a Piece of Sh--’: Jensen Huang’s Last Hours as Nvidia CEO – Bloomberg [ https://substack.com/redirect/b4d31547-641e-425d-8505-8ad71af74d93?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]
Bloomberg has a pretty wild account of Jensen’s final days as CEO. After weeks of denying the affair allegations and trying to stay as the company’s CEO, he reportedly confessed to the affair with Alice Huang after general counsel confronted him, then was forced to resign once the New York Times leaked information of his affair with Kimberly Powell. People described him as drained, saying he would respond to questions with “whatever” and “I don’t care” and calling himself a “POS.”
I’m sure more news will drop over the weekend. If so, you’ll hear our thoughts Monday morning.
Clip Spotlight: "Outside of NVIDIA, the biggest loser is the open-source ecosystem." — Dylan Patel
"Open-source in America would not exist without Jensen. Both Reflection and Thinking Machines are pushing the open-source frontier in America, and both have massive investments and backing from NVIDIA. As do many other companies across the ecosystem."
"And all these application companies rely on open models. Whether it's the Harveys, the Instincts, the Sierras — they all use open models."
"All the inference providers, which include multiple of the 10+ billion dollar companies, rely on open models from China."
"And if we're slamming the hammer down on China way harder, and the biggest defender against restrictions was Jensen and his team, what happens now? Given their credibility is out the window and they're being investigated and subpoenaed, etc."
"If there are going to be a lot more restrictions, do Chinese models continue to progress at the current pace? And if they do, do they open-source everything? These are real questions. But I think the open-source ecosystem is the biggest loser, for sure, if you're talking about outside of NVIDIA."
