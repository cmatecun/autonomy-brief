## AI Execs May Be Too Out of Touch to Build for the General Public

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/1ad041b7-5d9d-46a4-88e4-33941d7ba0e8/GettyImages-1172241499.jpeg?t=1793219328)
Caption: Image Credits: Nhat V. Meyer / Bay Area News Group / Getty Images

**By Amanda Silberling**

Former Yahoo CEO and early Google engineer Marissa Mayer is catching a lot of flak for how she uses AI. In [_an interview_](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/25/style/marissa-mayer-ai-assistants-women.html) about her new app [_Dazzle_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/with-dazzle-marissa-mayer-bets-your-camera-roll-has-more-info-on-your-life-than-your-inbox/), an AI assistant that learns from your camera roll, Mayer poses what she believes is an impressive flex for the product: It helped her come up with three bullet points about her 10-year-old daughter for a birthday presentation at school, describing her as “empathetic, creative, and an animal lover.”

It’s a vague description, like a generic horoscope — it could probably describe a lot of 10-year-olds.

Some onlookers, like progressive activist Leah Greenberg, [_marveled_](https://x.com/Leahgreenb/status/2114537457051897889) that “the fact that Marissa Mayer would voluntarily disclose this to a reporter is a pretty good demonstration of how deeply the culture of Silicon Valley has diverged from the rest of humanity.”

Thread:  (https://bsky.app/profile/rmac.bsky.social/post/3myrgnthtm22q)

Perhaps Mayer drew so much backlash because we have repeatedly observed sheltered billionaires suggest that we outsource [_child-rearing_](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/18/kid-pilled-sam-altman-constantly-asked-chatgpt-questions-about-his-newborn/) — one of life’s greatest tests of patience, humility, and kindness — to a computer that is [_incapable of understanding_](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/09/we-cant-help-treating-ai-like-its-human-but-should-we/) human emotion.

We’ve been primed for this frustration. Last year, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman [_told_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qMAg8_yf9zA) [_Bored Ape owner_](https://www.latimes.com/business/technology/story/2022-01-26/jimmy-fallon-nft-ape-nbc) and late night host Jimmy Fallon on national television, “I cannot imagine … figuring out how to raise a newborn without ChatGPT.” The public didn’t love that comment, either, but Altman wasn’t fazed by the [_negative_](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/sam-altman-parenting-strategy-horrifying) feedback. A few months ago, he said on X that a “[_cool use case_](https://x.com/sama/status/2083221585792762171)” of ChatGPT Work would be creating a podcast that talks about your kids’ upcoming birthdays or soccer games and “explain[s] your kids’ interests.” (He was brutally [_ratioed_](https://www.merriam-webster.com/slang/ratio) with [_comments_](https://x.com/_AlexHirsch/status/2083268104742924484) like, “What if you just talked to your children?”)

Read more (https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/ai-execs-may-be-too-out-of-touch-to-build-for-the-general-public/)
