Apple’s ‘Pro and Tell’
Apple is holding a special event today in Los Angeles after already announcing new M6 iMacs, a 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro, and an OLED iPad mini. The big reveal was Apple’s first touchscreen MacBook. The 14-inch is $2,899, and the 16-inch is $3,399. We’ll have Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman on the show today at 12p PT to break it all down.
Genie 4
Google DeepMind unveiled Genie 4 [ https://substack.com/redirect/e9eae39b-4c6e-4e48-a0bf-6d0d227db78f?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], its newest world model. The most noticeable improvement is that movement looks way cleaner and more natural instead of looking glitchy and robotic. Sundar posted [ https://substack.com/redirect/cd4f34ec-3b1e-4868-9b7c-794523d2ffea?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] a demo of a character on a motorcycle for reference. Google also just released [ https://substack.com/redirect/078d54a1-4b20-489a-8429-8e7796543ee9?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] its latest model Gemini 4 Carbon.
Monkey Business
8VC’s Joe Lonsdale is backing Safer Human Medicine [ https://substack.com/redirect/f2c9d349-f21d-42bf-b5c6-c317565e4d62?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], a startup that wants to build a domestic breeding operation capable of supplying 30,000 monkeys for biomedical research. Even Citrini is talking about the shortage of lab monkeys.
Anthropic’s Cash Pile
Anthropic disclosed [ https://substack.com/redirect/bcc33599-d4a9-49bf-9f84-d4d68ed93a4f?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] in its S-1 that it had $112.4B in cash and another $19.3B in short-term investments as of June, totaling $131.7B. That’s more than every non-bank U.S. company except Berkshire Hathaway and Alphabet, and the IPO could add tens of billions more.
Clip Spotlight: Gavin Baker on neolabs pivoting into neoclouds:
“If you signed a long-term compute contract at any point prior to May of 2026, you are way in the money. You’re buying compute for $10 or $15 a watt. And you could sell it at a massive premium to that price today.”
“And it’s not just that the spot market is tight today. You have to pay vast amounts to bring on a cluster in mid- to late-2027.”
“I think that’s something that public markets are not appreciating... All [this information] is out there, but for whatever reason, I think it’s not legible to the public markets.”
