Top Story of the Week
DoorDash reveals launch timeframe for delivery drones in Northern California (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/35a05172-a6a3-45a5-b1d3-36ac1e2277c4?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).
Trial runs of the drone deliveries will begin in the coming months in Northern California in partnership with national restaurant brands including Chipotle and Popeyes. The drone delivery program aims to combat the hardest part of autonomous delivery: the first and last ten feet. Picking up an order from a kitchen and laying it safely on the customer’s doorstep are the most challenging steps in the food delivery process, according to DoorDash.

Robotaxi operators will face fines for blocking first responders (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/29e3e2a1-09ae-4824-892b-95667b7f75cc?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]). Thirty minutes feels like a pretty generous window if an AV is actually blocking police, fire or EMS. The remote driver provision is also narrower than it sounds: it only applies when a human can directly control the vehicle from afar. Waymo says its remote staff provide assistance while the AV system stays in control, so its overseas remote assistance teams wouldn’t appear to be affected by this requirement.
California’s new law will allow AV operators to be fined if a vehicle blocks first responders for more than 30 minutes, although the DMV can set shorter response-time requirements. Under the law, AV developers can also only employ remote drivers who are based in the United States and hold a U.S. driver’s license. The requirement is aimed at concerns around how companies use remote operations when autonomous vehicles run into trouble.

Minneapolis City Council passes divisive ordinance requiring human drivers in driverless vehicles (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/d8b5c4a2-c02d-4839-9bb1-aa753684ec2a?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], no paywall [ https://substack.com/redirect/b3fb6739-52cc-4869-bdc3-21e2b39ff7ec?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).
Along with requiring any company running a robotaxi service to hold a city license, it would require a “human safety monitor” in the driver’s seat with immediate access to “steering, braking, and acceleration.” It wouldn’t go into effect until October 2027.
Kodiak Picks Dallas-Houston For Driverless Long-Haul Launch (link [ https://substack.com/redirect/6ad6d879-aae8-4840-9c2f-33bf5b54155a?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).
