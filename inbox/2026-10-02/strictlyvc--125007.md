You’re officially in — welcome to _StrictlyVC_. Thanks for confirming your subscription.

Here’s what to expect:

We send out a free daily newsletter, Monday through Friday (except holidays), to keep you informed about what’s happening in the world of venture capital and startups — in Silicon Valley and beyond. We cover the money, the movers, the missteps, and the moments that matter.

Alongside the newsletter, we host special evening events that bring together VCs, founders, and corporate development execs. Past guests include Sam Altman (OpenAI), Marc Andreessen (a16z), Katie Haun (Haun Ventures), Lina Khan (former FTC chair), and Alexis Ohanian (Reddit, 776).  

As for us, I’m a former writer for _Red Herring_ magazine who has worked in venture capital and corporate development, and Connie is the GM and Editor-in-Chief of _TechCrunch_ and the founder of _StrictlyVC_. She has been covering the venture scene since her own time at the _Red Herring_. 

Thanks again for joining us. We’re glad to have you here.
