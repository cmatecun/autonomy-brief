Cars and Houses
You Can See Everything, the new Nathan Fielder movie about Elizabeth Homes is as incredible as everyone says. Highly recommend seeing it. I won’t spoil anything here, but we will probably talk about it a little more loosely on the show today, so beware if you want to be completely spoiler free. Also, the house [ https://substack.com/redirect/d4bd65d7-5b52-495d-a073-835d6ac6a2eb?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ] where they filmed most of the documentary is on Zillow.
Yesterday was also the launch of the Porsche Mission S and the new Tesla Roadster. Tons of debate over these, I think they both look great. The Roadster event definitely lived up to the hype, the demo car does fly, it kickflips, it goes zero to sixty in under a second, it’s crazy, but it’s also delayed again, with production starting in 2028 (which could of course slip). I was really hoping that the lower trim level (no rockets) would ship within 6 months, but it seems like everyone will have to wait. The response was overwhelmingly positive on X though, with people not really caring about price or timelines here. I do think even if it takes 10 years, it’s good to have founders focused on really long horizon projects, it’s inspiring. There are two directions I could see the Roadster ultimately going (and it seems like they might try and do both, but I do think they will be at odds). First, it beat every track record, that means sub 5 minutes and 19 seconds on the Nürburgring to be the Porsche 919 Hybrid Evo (insane). Second, is actually figuring out how to deploy the “flying” capability in a useful way (a tall order).
We will be debating all show long.
