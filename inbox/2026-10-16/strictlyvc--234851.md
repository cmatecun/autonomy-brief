## Mark Wahlberg Wants to Take on the Longevity-Startup Crowd With an Affordable Gym Concept

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/9356a308-a36e-46c6-b2fc-8d25b08ba779/Mark-Wahlberg.jpeg?t=1792205994)
Caption: Image Credits: Kimberly White / Getty Images

**By Connie Loizos**

Mark Wahlberg has started companies of his own and backed plenty of others. But the gym he’s opening in December, he says, has him more excited than anything he’s done in his entire career. It also goes after a corner of the market where venture money keeps flowing: pricey, diagnostics-heavy wellness.

Speaking with us at TechCrunch Disrupt in San Francisco on Thursday, the actor said the first Municipal Gym opens in December. It’s a 32,000-square-foot club outside Las Vegas with blood work, epigenetic testing, recovery gear, compression therapy, cold plunges, and a café that serves up protein muffins, among other things. He’s pitching it as the affordable alternative to the longevity clubs that charge tens of thousands of dollars a year for much the same thing.

It’s the second part of a scheme that was, in some ways, first hatched seven years ago, with a casual sportswear line.

The clothing brand Municipal launched during COVID, which Wahlberg volunteers “was not a good idea.” (Clothing was a lousy business to enter in 2020, with retailers from [J.Crew](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/j-crew-files-for-chap-11-as-pandemic-chokes-retail) to [Brooks Brothers](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/brooks-brothers-bankruptcy-coronavirus-pandemic-struggles/) going bankrupt.)

It grew out of what he’d been seeing as a celebrity fitness spokesperson. He’d done collabs with various brands, and other companies kept approaching him to be their brand ambassador, but they all seemed to want the same thing, he said. “Everybody just kind of wanted to sell T-shirts and sneakers. And I was like, well, I want to kind of have a bigger impact on people’s lives as a whole.”

By that, he meant what people eat, what supplements they take, how they train, and how they recover. Clothing was just the start. “We figured, okay, we’ll start in the apparel space, and then we’ll build from there.” He says he only takes swings at “things that I’m very passionate about and things that are a direct extension of who I am.”

## Massive Fundings

**E****stuary Biotherapeutics**, a four-year-old Menlo Park startup that develops gene therapies delivered directly to the kidneys to treat inherited kidney diseases, raised a $103 million Series A round led by **RA Capital Management**, with **Deep Track Capital**, **Biogen New Ventures**, **UPMC Enterprises**, **Pivotal bioVenture Partners**, and the **National Kidney Foundation Innovation Fund** as well as previous investors **4BIO Capital** and **GV** also digging in. BioPharma Dive has _[more here](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/estuary-kidney-disease-gene-therapy-launch-emerged-biogen-biotech-startup/833114/)_.

**Rabot Energy**, a five-year-old Hamburg startup that offers dynamic electricity tariffs and coordinates household batteries to store cheaper renewable power and balance the grid, raised a $56.1 million Series B round co-led by **Endeit** and **DeepTech & Climate Fund**, with **vent.io** as well as previous investors **HV Capital**, **High-Tech Gründerfonds**, **Bosch Ventures**, **CNI**, and **Acurio Ventures** also stepping up. Tech Funding News has _[more here](https://techfundingnews.com/rabot-energy-raises-50m-series-b-virtual-power-plant-endeit-capital/)_.

**Stand**, a three-year-old San Francisco startup that helps homeowners strengthen and insure properties in disaster-prone regions using simulations of wildfire, wind, and other hazards, raised a $70 million Series C round at a $1 billion valuation. The deal was led by **Saga Ventures**, with additional support from **Lowercarbon**, **Eclipse**, **Equal Ventures**, **Inspired Capital**, **Neotribe**, and **Jackson Square Ventures**. The company has raised a total of $130 million. _[More here](https://fintech.global/2026/10/16/stand-hits-1bn-valuation-with-70m-series-c-raise/)_.
