On that note, here were a few of our favorites this week.

* *What is Apple Doing?* On last week’s Dithering, ( https://dithering.passport.online/member/episode/apples-2010-s-mistakes ) John and I bemoaned Apple’s exit from home appliances in the 2010s; now the company is back and John doesn’t see the point ( https://dithering.passport.online/member/episode/home-view ). I think a big problem was Apple’s marketing video; after I explained what Apple was trying to accomplish on Sharp Tech ( https://sharptech.fm/member/episode/apple-enters-the-uncanny-valley-the-space-x-play-for-cellular-q-a-on-the-ringer-smartphones-ai-parenting ) Andrew was almost persuaded to go all in on HomeView. — Ben Thompson

* *SpaceX Continues Eating and Expands Its Appetite.* Elon Musk and SpaceX have continued their spectrum buying spree, and in Tuesday’s update ( https://stratechery.com/2026/spacex-buys-more-spectrum-musk-and-market-structure-distributed-cellular/ ) Ben broke down the implications of both the latest deal, as well as recent deals that mobile carriers (and airlines) have done to avoid reliance on SpaceX. This market’s dynamics are fascinating to me, and for SpaceX, what once looked like a play to become the de-facto provider of emergency satellite coverage among mobile providers now looks like a company that’s coming for primary cellular coverage. Come to read about the methods that could make that path viable, and stay to appreciate challenges that make it a quintessential Musk strategy. — Andrew Sharp

* *Titles, Takes, and the Washington Wizards.* The NBA season begins next Tuesday, and if you want to have a good time preparing this weekend, we’ve got you covered. First, Greatest of All Talk makes the case ( https://goat.passport.online/member/episode/tell-me-why-they-will-preseason-highlight-digest-sizing-up-a-loaded-northwest-division ) for eight different potential title winners (although I must warn you that our Knicks repeat case was extremely unconvincing) and runs down our favorite highlights from the preseason. Then, for Friday’s Sharp Text ( https://sharptext.net/2026/a-wizards-public-service-announcement-and-18-more-nba-takes/ ) , I played the most dangerous game of all and expressed confidence the Washington Wizards (for the first time in a full decade) while also offering 18 more takes and predictions for the rest of the league. — AS
