In today’s episode, I’m speaking with Ethan McKanna [ https://substack.com/redirect/c6bfac8f-84b7-4817-be7e-b0673e8ba6fb?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], an autonomous vehicle power user, industry observer, and the creator of Robotaxi Tracker [ https://substack.com/redirect/ea6adfd5-20df-4ebe-9423-d23cbd47aac1?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. Ethan returns to the podcast to share what inspired him to build the platform and how he gathers data to track autonomous vehicle deployments.
We discuss the metrics Robotaxi Tracker covers, how Tesla’s fleet numbers compare with Waymo’s, and the methods Ethan uses to identify and count vehicles. Ethan also explains some of the common misinterpretations of the data and the steps he takes to help people understand what the numbers actually tell us.
We also explore how companies, cities, and the press interact with Robotaxi Tracker, and how the platform could help cities better understand autonomous vehicle activity on their streets. Ethan shares how much time goes into maintaining the project, his thinking around monetization, and what could make the data more accurate.
Finally, we discuss potential new features, why utilization is such an anticipated metric, and what Ethan’s data reveals about other operators, including Zoox and Avride.
