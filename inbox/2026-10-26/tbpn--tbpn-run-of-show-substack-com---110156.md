The Most Interesting Facts from Anthropic’s S-1
Anthropic submitted its S-1 this morning. Here are some of the most interesting facts:
$67.2B | Annualized Revenue: Up 45% QoQ, (full Q3 numbers aren’t in the initial S-1).
$131.7B | Cash on Hand: June 30, 2026 balance, super capital efficient.
$110.8B | H1 financing cash inflows: Primarily redeemable convertible preferred equity.
$72.4B | Noncash financing losses: Fair-value losses on convertible notes.
$482M | H1 capital expenditures: Remarkably low, reflects rented cloud strategy.
72% → 48% | Compute costs / revenue: Rapidly improving efficiency QoQ.
−13% → +19% | Adjusted operating margin: 32% gain QoQ, almost $1b in GAAP profit.
11,900+ | Customers spending $100K+: Up from approximately 950 a year earlier.
900% | Net revenue retention: extraordinary expansion in usage per customer.
50%+ | Fortune 500 penetration: Over half spend > $100k annually.
-20% | Holiday spending decline: Christmas season hits hard on token usage.
$568M → $3.17B | Marketing budget from 2025 to H1 2026, just $2M in 2023
The company plans to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT the week of November 16th.
Anthropic’s founders pledged to keep ads out of Claude.
Some of the risks outlined in the S-1 include Anthropic’s $125B equipment lease with Broadcom, 48% of revenue coming through cloud marketplaces, its founders having 50.1% of voting power, and AI accelerating beyond its limits to control it.
We’ll be unpacking all of this on the show today.
