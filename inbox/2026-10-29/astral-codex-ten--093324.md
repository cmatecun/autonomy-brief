20: AI Futures Project on how the US could “pace the frontier” of AI [ https://substack.com/redirect/f2000e5e-68bf-4777-8241-8256141d00ef?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]. Suppose the US has a six month lead over China. That means they have the option to pause for 5.99 months (and use that time to focus on safety) without China breaking ahead. The conventional wisdom is that if they’re going to do this, they should do it at the last possible moment, so that they have the best possible AIs to help with their safety research. AIFP breaks with that and suggests pausing now. Why? US companies currently have such bad security that China can easily steal any advances we make; therefore, continuing to race buys us no extra lead (China’s continued handicap comes down to having less compute, which is harder to steal). We can spend the pause increasing our security along with safety research and other things, and then race later, if we have to (they continue to prefer a mutual pacing deal with Beijing [ https://substack.com/redirect/4fdeef4b-ab27-4d32-a905-e92142f0989e?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ]).

22: Bill Gates on climate [ https://substack.com/redirect/3f5174b5-458a-474e-ab0d-8990af950b5c?j=eyJ1IjoiMXlnZjcifQ.KOUIeBfeCRzhIwFnKXyQigR4j0VpF-SZzascYhSZunw ], hopefully self-recommending. My summary:

Is this true, and, if so, how could it be possible?
